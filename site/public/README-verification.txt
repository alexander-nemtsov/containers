Search-engine ownership verification files live in this directory.

The upstream minimalcontainers.com verification token was removed with the
rebrand. When catalog.nexaflow.by is connected to GitHub Pages and registered
in Search Console / other webmaster tools, drop the new verification file(s)
here so they are served at the site root.

Verification tokens are not secrets. They prove control of the site to the
search engine and grant nothing to anyone who reads them.
